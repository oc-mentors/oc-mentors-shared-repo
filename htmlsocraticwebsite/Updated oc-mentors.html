Privacy Policy for Socratic OC

Introduction

Socratic OC ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of our users, including high school students aged 13-17 and their parents. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and share personal and educational information. By using our website, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of personal and educational information:

  • • Personal Information: Name, email address, and phone number.
  • • Educational Information: Academic records, assignments, grades, and other data provided by students, parents, or schools.
  • • Usage Data: Non-identifiable information such as browser type, pages visited, and time spent on the site.
  • • Cookies and Tracking Technologies: Data collected through cookies and similar technologies to enhance user experience.

Purposes for Collection

We collect and use your information for the following purposes:

  1. 1. Educational Services: To provide updates, educational content, and events related to Socratic OC.
  2. 2. Account Management: To create and manage user accounts, including parental controls.
  3. 3. Analytics and Improvements: To analyze website usage and improve our services.
  4. 4. Compliance: To comply with legal obligations, including FERPA, COPPA, and CCPA/CPRA requirements.

How We Protect Your Information

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your personal and educational information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. These measures include encryption, secure servers, and access controls.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal or educational information. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • • With Service Providers: To trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating our website and delivering services.
  • • With Schools or Educational Institutions: To comply with FERPA requirements and provide educational services.
  • • As Required by Law: To comply with legal obligations, such as responding to subpoenas or court orders.

Your Rights

Under applicable laws, you have the following rights regarding your personal and educational information:

  1. 1. Access: You may request access to the information we have collected about you.
  2. 2. Correction: You may request corrections to inaccurate or incomplete information.
  3. 3. Deletion: You may request the deletion of your personal information, subject to certain exceptions under the law.
  4. 4. Opt-Out: If you are aged 13-16, you have the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at octutoringservice@gmail.com.

Parental Rights and Consent

For users under the age of 18, parents or legal guardians have the right to:

  • • Review the information collected about their child.
  • • Request the deletion of their child's information.
  • • Refuse further collection or use of their child's information.

We require verifiable parental consent before collecting personal information from users under the age of 13, in compliance with COPPA.

Data Retention

We retain personal and educational information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy or as required by law. Once the retention period expires, we securely delete or anonymize the data.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at octutoringservice@gmail.com
