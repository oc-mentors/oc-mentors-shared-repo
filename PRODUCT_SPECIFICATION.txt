1. PRODUCT SPECIFICATION DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Provide a specific description about your product's functionality and purpose, 
focusing on the following:

• Who are the users of this product?

The users of this product include all college students, with a specific emphasis 
on students who are in need of academic support. The platform also serves tutors, 
TAs, and instructors who provide that support, as well as administrators who 
manage the system. Students can use the app as learners; tutors and TAs use it 
to manage availability, view students, and track analytics.

• What can a user of the product accomplish with it and how?

A user of our software can organize all of their coursework, plan study sessions, 
connect with TAs, tutors, and other people to help them achieve their academic 
goals. They can track their progress as a student and they can schedule online 
or in-person tutoring sessions. Our app is an all-in-one student help app. 
Specifically, users can:

  - Browse and search tutors by subject and learning style, view profiles and 
    reviews, and book one-on-one or recurring tutoring sessions.
  - Connect their institution’s Canvas LMS account to see courses, assignments, 
    and announcements in one place without switching between Canvas and the app.
  - Use an in-app schedule and calendar to view upcoming sessions, past lessons, 
    and study sessions, and join video sessions (e.g., Zoom/Google Meet) from 
    the app.
  - Message tutors, TAs, and peers through an in-app chat with support for 
    attachments.
  - Complete a learning style quiz and get matched with tutors whose teaching 
    style fits their preferences (e.g., Visual, Auditory, Reading/Writing, 
    Kinesthetic).
  - Track academic progress by subject, view stats (lessons taken, hours studied, 
    GPA), complete tasks, and earn achievements.
  - Customize the experience with theme and accent color options (e.g., light/dark).
  - Rate and review completed sessions to help other students choose tutors.

Tutors can manage their availability, view their students, and see analytics; 
they get a dedicated tutor home and tutor-specific routes.

• What are the major operations it can perform?

Major operations include:

  - Scheduling, managing, and recording online or in-person tutoring sessions 
    (book, cancel, complete, join via video link).
  - Integrating Canvas information (courses, assignments, announcements, 
    calendar events) via the Canvas LMS API (OAuth2) to bring a simpler and 
    more organized user experience for students who may find Canvas cluttered 
    and hard to navigate.
  - A messaging system between students, TAs, and tutors, with conversations, 
    real-time updates, and file/image attachments.
  - An in-app schedule and calendar to help students stay organized (upcoming 
    sessions, past lessons, study sessions, recurring classes).
  - User authentication (register, login, profile) and role-based access 
    (student, tutor, admin).
  - Progress tracking (by subject, tasks, achievements, stats) and a learning 
    style quiz with tutor matching.
  - Reviews and ratings for completed sessions.
  - Recommended resources and a personalized learning plan (e.g., “Start 
    Today’s Learning Plan”).
  - Theme customization and notification/course notification settings.

• What is the user interface?

The user interface is a modern, responsive web application built with React. 
It is designed for use on desktop and mobile browsers (with a mobile-first, 
single-column layout capped at max-width for larger screens).

  - Navigation: A bottom navigation bar gives quick access to five main areas: 
    Home, Tutors (browse/book tutors), Schedule (calendar and sessions), Chat 
    (messages), and Canvas (linked courses, assignments, announcements). 
    Additional entry points include Profile, Settings, Progress, and 
    Learning Style Quiz. Tutor users see a dedicated Tutor Home and links to 
    Tutor Students, Tutor Availability, and Tutor Analytics.

  - Layout and design: The UI uses a clean, card-based layout with clear 
    typography and spacing. Key surfaces include a personalized dashboard 
    (e.g., “Hi, [Name]”, today’s plan, next session countdown), subject 
    cards with icons and course colors, and lists for meetings, resources, 
    and assignments. Buttons use an accent color (customizable) with subtle 
    shadows and hover states. Motion (e.g., Framer Motion) is used for 
    gentle animations on page load and interactions.

  - Key screens and flows: 
    - Splash and Login/Register for authentication.
    - Home: greeting, “Start Today’s Learning Plan” CTA, today’s plan, 
      upcoming meetings, subject shortcuts, recommended resources.
    - Tutors: search, filters by subject, tutor cards (avatar, name, 
      subject, rating, price), tutor detail with bio, availability, 
      reviews, and “Book session.”
    - Schedule: calendar view and list of upcoming/past sessions with 
      options to join video, rate session, or view details.
    - Chat: conversation list (with last message and unread state) and 
      conversation view with message bubbles and attachments.
    - Canvas: Canvas login/link flow, Canvas classes list, course-specific 
      announcements and assignments (with due dates and status).
    - Progress: subject progress, tasks, achievements, and stats.
    - Learning Style Quiz: multiple-choice questions and result with 
      learning style and matching.
    - Settings: profile edit, theme customization, notifications, and 
      Canvas connection status.
  - Theming: Support for light and dark themes and user-selectable accent 
    colors so the interface can match institutional or user preference.

  - Technology: The UI is implemented with React (and React Router), 
    TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Radix UI and MUI components, Lucide 
    icons, Recharts for charts, and Motion for animations. There are no 
    native mobile apps; the product is a web app accessible via browser.

2. PRODUCT SPECIFICATION — SOFTWARE AND HARDWARE REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Describe the software/hardware requirements of your product. For example, 
provide the operating system, 3rd party applications, and/or physical 
components to make your product functional.

SOFTWARE REQUIREMENTS

  • Operating system (end user):
    - Any modern OS that supports a current web browser: Windows 10/11, 
      macOS 10.15+, Linux (recent distributions), iOS 14+, or Android 8+.
    - No specific OS is required; the product runs in the browser.

  • Web browser (end user):
    - A modern, standards-compliant browser with JavaScript enabled:
      Chrome/Edge (recommended), Firefox, Safari, or equivalent.
    - Cookies and local storage enabled for authentication and preferences 
      (e.g., theme, Canvas connection status).

  • Development / deployment (for running the project):
    - Node.js (LTS version, e.g., 18.x or 20.x) and npm (or pnpm) for 
      installing dependencies and running the Vite dev server and build.
    - A code editor or IDE (e.g., VS Code, Cursor) is optional for 
      development only.

  • Backend and third-party services:
    - Firebase (Google): Used for authentication (email/password and optional 
      providers), Cloud Firestore (users, tutors, sessions, conversations, 
      messages, progress, reviews, resources), and Firebase Storage (avatars, 
      chat attachments). A Firebase project must be created and configured 
      (API key, auth domain, project ID, storage bucket, messaging sender ID, 
      app ID) via environment variables (e.g., .env with VITE_FIREBASE_*).
    - Canvas LMS (Instructure): For Canvas integration, the institution must 
      expose the Canvas REST API and OAuth2 (authorization and token endpoints). 
      The app uses OAuth2; it does not collect or store Canvas passwords. 
      End users need a Canvas account at a supported institution.

  • Optional / future:
    - Video conferencing: Integration with a third-party service (e.g., Zoom 
      or Google Meet) for “Join” links in sessions; no specific client 
      installation required beyond the browser.
    - (Optional) Cloud Functions for scheduled Canvas sync or server-side 
      tasks; would require a Firebase Blaze plan if used.

  • Build and tooling (included in the project):
    - Vite 6.x (build and dev server).
    - React 18.x, React Router 7.x, TypeScript.
    - Tailwind CSS 4.x, Radix UI, MUI (Material UI) components, Lucide React, 
      Motion (animation), Recharts, date-fns, react-hook-form, and other 
      dependencies listed in package.json.

HARDWARE REQUIREMENTS

  • End user:
    - A device (PC, laptop, tablet, or smartphone) with a display and 
      internet connection capable of running a modern web browser.
    - For video tutoring: microphone and camera (usually built-in or USB) 
      and a stable internet connection; actual video runs in the browser 
      or in the linked third-party video app.

  • No dedicated server or special hardware is required for the product 
    itself; backend services are provided by Firebase and the institution’s 
    Canvas instance.

3. PROPOSED DEMONSTRATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Use this section to describe your proposed product demo. The demo should be a 
working prototype of some key functionality of the product that you are 
designing and illustrate the competitive differentiation and/or technology 
that you are relying on. This is just a “proposed demo.”

PROPOSED DEMO OVERVIEW

The demonstration will use the existing OC Mentors web application as a 
working prototype. The demo will show a short, scripted flow that highlights 
core value for students and the main differentiators: (1) Canvas integration 
for a single, less cluttered view of courses and assignments, (2) tutor 
discovery and session scheduling, (3) in-app messaging, and (4) progress 
and personalization (learning style and theme). The goal is to show a 
cohesive “all-in-one” student help experience in one product.

COMPETITIVE DIFFERENTIATION AND TECHNOLOGY

  • Canvas integration: Many students juggle Canvas and separate tutoring 
    or planning tools. OC Mentors connects to Canvas via OAuth2 and surfaces 
    courses, assignments (with due dates and status), and announcements 
    inside the app. The demo will show linking Canvas (or a simulated state), 
    then viewing “Canvas classes” and assignments in one place without 
    leaving the app.

  • Unified scheduling and sessions: Students can browse tutors by subject, 
    see availability, book one-off or recurring sessions, and view everything 
    on an in-app schedule with options to join video sessions. The demo will 
    show the flow: Home → Tutors → select a tutor → Book session → Schedule 
    view with the session and a “Join” (video) option.

  • Messaging in one place: Communication with tutors and TAs happens inside 
    the app. The demo will show the Chat list and a sample conversation to 
    illustrate that students don’t need to switch to email or external chat 
    apps.

  • Personalization: The learning style quiz and theme customization show 
    that the app adapts to the user (matching learning style and visual 
    preference). The demo can briefly show the quiz result and theme 
    settings to support this.

DEMO FLOW (PROPOSED)

  1. Opening: Start on Splash or Login; log in as a student (pre-created 
     account).

  2. Home: Show the dashboard with greeting, “Start Today’s Learning Plan” 
     button, today’s plan, and upcoming sessions. Emphasize one place for 
     plan and next session.

  3. Canvas: Go to the Canvas section; show Canvas login/link (or that it’s 
     already connected). Open Canvas classes and then assignments (or 
     announcements) to show coursework and due dates in-app.

  4. Tutors and booking: Go to Tutors, apply a subject filter or search, 
     open a tutor profile (bio, rating, availability), and book a session 
     (date/time). Show the session appearing on Schedule.

  5. Schedule: Open Schedule; show the calendar/list with the newly booked 
     session and a “Join” or “Video” action to illustrate online session 
     access.

  6. Chat: Open Chat, show conversation list and one conversation with 
     messages (and optional attachment) to show in-app messaging with 
     tutors/TAs.

  7. (Optional) Progress: Briefly show Progress (e.g., subject progress or 
     achievements) and/or Learning Style Quiz result to reinforce 
     tracking and personalization.

  8. (Optional) Settings: Show theme customization (light/dark, accent 
     color) to illustrate user control over the interface.

DELIVERABLES FOR THE DEMO

  • A running instance of the web app (local or deployed), with Firebase 
    configured and (if possible) a test Canvas OAuth2 app or sandbox for 
    the Canvas steps.
  • Pre-created test accounts: at least one student and one tutor, with 
    sample sessions, messages, and (if available) Canvas-linked data.
  • A stable internet connection and a browser (Chrome/Edge recommended) 
    for the live demo; backup: short screen recording of the same flow.

This proposed demonstration shows a working prototype of key functionality 
and clearly illustrates the product’s differentiation (Canvas integration, 
unified scheduling and messaging, and personalization) and the use of 
modern web and cloud technology (React, Firebase, Canvas API).
